Why the old tram line is coming back

By Ilse Verhoeven, 3 November 2026

After forty years of buses, the city council voted on Tuesday to rebuild the Line 7 tram between the station and the harbour.

Construction is planned to start in 2028. Supporters say the route will cut travel time by a third; opponents worry about the cost of 85 million euros.

"It is the first time in a generation that we invest in rails," said councillor Mo Haddad. The plan goes to a public hearing in January.

Residents along the harbour road will receive a letter with the proposed timetable before the end of the year.
