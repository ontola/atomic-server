Lesson 4: Fractions on the number line

Grade 5, about 45 minutes.

Learning goals

  • Place unit fractions and simple fractions on a number line.
  • Compare fractions with the same denominator.
  • Explain why 1/2 and 2/4 are equal.

Activities

Warm-up: fold a paper strip in halves and quarters. Main task: pupils mark 1/4, 1/2 and 3/4 on a strip and compare with a partner. Closing: exit ticket with three questions.

Materials

Paper strips, rulers, coloured pencils.
