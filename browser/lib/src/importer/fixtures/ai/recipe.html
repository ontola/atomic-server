Slow tomato soup with basil

A calm weeknight soup that gets better the next day. Serves 4, takes about 50 minutes, most of it unattended.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 500 ml vegetable stock
  • A handful of basil
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Roast the tomatoes, onion and garlic with the oil at 200 C for 30 minutes.
  2. Tip everything into a pot, add the stock and simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth, stir in the basil, season and serve.

Prep: 10 minutes. Cook: 40 minutes.
