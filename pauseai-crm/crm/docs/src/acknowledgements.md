# Acknowledgements

## Funding

Atomic Data and AtomicServer have been supported by public research and innovation funding, which
is a large part of why this project is MIT licensed and has no proprietary core.

- **[Atomic Data](https://nlnet.nl/project/AtomicData/)** (2021-2023), the specification and the
  first versions of AtomicServer, was funded through the
  [NGI Assure Fund](https://nlnet.nl/assure), a fund established by [NLnet](https://nlnet.nl) with
  financial support from the European Commission's [Next Generation Internet](https://ngi.eu)
  programme, under the aegis of DG Communications Networks, Content and Technology under grant
  agreement No 957073.
- **[Atomic Tables](https://nlnet.nl/project/AtomicTables/)** (2022-2023), the typed collaborative
  table editor, was funded through the [NGI0 Entrust Fund](https://nlnet.nl/entrust), a fund
  established by NLnet with financial support from the European Commission's Next Generation
  Internet programme, under the aegis of DG Communications Networks, Content and Technology under
  grant agreement No 101069594.
- **AtomicServer Local-First** (2025-2026), the local-first foundation described throughout these
  docs (Loro CRDTs, `did:ad` decentralized identifiers, the binary v2 sync protocol, direct
  device-to-device sync over Iroh, content-addressed binary data, and serverless peer discovery),
  was funded through the [NGI0 Commons Fund](https://nlnet.nl/commonsfund/), a fund established by
  NLnet with financial support from the European Commission's Next Generation Internet programme,
  under the aegis of DG Communications Networks, Content and Technology under grant agreement
  No 101092990.
- Further work has been supported through
  [Eurostars](https://www.eurekanetwork.org/programmes-and-calls/eurostars/), the Eureka
  programme for research-performing SMEs, co-funded by the participating national funding
  agencies and the European Union.

We are grateful for that support, and for NLnet's approach to it in particular: they fund open
technology without asking for anything proprietary in return, and they gave us the room to
evaluate a technical direction honestly and then change course when the evidence pointed
elsewhere.
